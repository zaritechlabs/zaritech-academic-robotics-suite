[ELECTROMECHANICAL WIRING DIAGRAM TRACK]

 1. POWER SUPPLY HARDWIRING:
    - 18650 Battery Holder POSITIVE (+) Wire ---> Breadboard VCC Rail (+5V)
    - 18650 Battery Holder NEGATIVE (-) Wire ---> Breadboard GND Rail (0V)

 2. COIL CONTROL INTERLOCK CIRCUIT:
    - Relay Module VCC  ---> Breadboard VCC Rail
    - Relay Module GND  ---> Breadboard GND Rail
    - Tact Button Pin 1A ---> Breadboard VCC Rail
    - Tact Button Pin 1B ---> Relay Module INPUT (IN) Pin
    * Note: Pressing the button routes current to open/close the inductive core.

 3. INDUCTIVE MOTOR DRIVE CIRCUIT:
    - Breadboard VCC Rail ---> Relay Module COMMON (COM) Terminal
    - Relay Module NORMALLY OPEN (NO) Terminal ---> DC Motor Terminal A (+)
    - DC Motor Terminal B (-) ---> Breadboard GND Rail
