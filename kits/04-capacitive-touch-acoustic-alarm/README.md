### ⚙️ Educational Paradigm: Computational-Free I/O Interlock & Haptic Signaling
Before developing complex micro-service software loops, engineers must master the relationship between hardware state inputs and physical auditory indicators. This academy suite isolates pure **Human-Machine Interface (HMI)** signaling logic. By linking a solid-state **Capacitive Touch Sensor Module** directly to a high-resonance **Active Buzzer Modül** on a unified mini breadboard backplane, students experiment with instantaneous signal transduction. Touch triggers a direct change in voltage state, forcing immediate acoustic telemetry generation without requiring complex firmware initialization steps.

## 🛠️ Hardware & Topology Specs

*   **Solid-State Input Node:** Capacitive Touch Proximity Sensor Module featuring a high-sensitivity contact copper substrate for clean digital high/low voltage shifting.
*   **Acoustic Actuation Terminal:** 5V High-Resonance Active Buzzer Module generating high-decibel square-wave tone alerts upon receiving high-level logic triggers.
